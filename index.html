Rabbit R1 creations

Two small apps for the R1 handheld, built for its 240 by 282 screen. Wheel to scroll, side button to select. Both run offline and store their state on the device.

Memory Grid

A sequence memory game. Watch the tiles light up, then repeat the pattern. Nine shapes, growing rounds, a best score that survives a restart.
